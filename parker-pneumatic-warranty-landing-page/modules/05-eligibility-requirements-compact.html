Warranty Eligibility Requirements

To qualify for the 5-year extended warranty, systems must meet these requirements:

1

All Parker Pneumatic Products Required

FRLs, actuators, and valves must all be Parker PDN products.

2

No Competitive Products Allowed

Can apply to individual machines with all Parker content.

3

Use Genuine Parker Filter Elements

Other manufacturer's elements void the warranty.

4

Coordinate with Parker Representative

Qualification is model-by-model and system-by-system.

5

Follow All Industry Standards

Install per specifications, guides, and technical info provided.

6

Establish Preventive Maintenance

Filter changes every 12 months (or sooner); maintain records.

7

Meet All Application Requirements

Performance, endurance, maintenance, safety, and warnings.

8

Extended Warranty Coverage Period

60 months from first use OR 66 months from delivery.

9

Governed by Parker Terms of Sale

See parker.com/salesterms for details.

10

Maintain Documentation

Keep proof of purchase for filter elements and PM records/logs.
