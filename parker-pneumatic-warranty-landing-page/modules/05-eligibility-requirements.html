Program Requirements

Warranty Eligibility Requirements

To qualify for the 5-year extended warranty, systems must meet specific design and installation standards. Review the requirements below to ensure your equipment is eligible.

Click each requirement to expand and learn more ↓

Equipment or machine must contain all Parker Pneumatic (PDN) products. Air Prep (FRLs), Actuators, and Valves must be all Parker PDN products to qualify for the extended warranty.

No competitive products may be used on equipment being qualified. The program can apply to individual machines within a larger system if that machine has all Parker PDN content.

Parker filter elements must be used. Any other manufacturer's elements will void the warranty. Keep records of filter purchases for warranty verification.

Qualification is handled model-by-model and system-by-system. Coordinate with your Parker representative to ensure proper enrollment and documentation.

All industry standards, specifications, and technical information provided with the quote or products (seller instructions, guides, specifications) must be followed for installation and operation.

A proper Preventive Maintenance (PM) plan must be established and followed. Parker recommends filter element change-out every 12 months (or sooner depending on your application). Maintain records of all maintenance activities.

All performance, endurance, maintenance, safety, and warning requirements of the application must be met. Ensure proper installation and operation within specified parameters.

Approved goods are warranted against defects in materials and workmanship for 60 months from first use or 66 months from delivery, whichever occurs first.

This warranty offer and acceptance are governed by the "Offer of Sale" provisions available at parker.com/salesterms.

Warranty claims follow existing Parker process and procedures. End users may need to provide proof of purchase of approved Parker filter elements and preventive maintenance records/logs showing filter replacement dates.
